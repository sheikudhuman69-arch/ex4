Lord Murugan (also known as Kartikeya, Subramanya, Skanda, Shanmuga) is the son of Lord Shiva and Goddess Parvati, and the god of valor, wisdom, and victory. Murugan temples are especially significant in Tamil Nadu, where He is regarded as the guardian deity of the Tamil people.
