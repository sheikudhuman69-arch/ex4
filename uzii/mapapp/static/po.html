Tenkasi

pottalpudhur dargah - My Home Town

Architecture: The dargah is notable for being built in a style similar to a Hindu temple. The design incorporates temple-like patterns and structural features. Rituals / Customs: Devotees of all religions (Muslims, Hindus, Christians) visit the dargah. “Nerchai” (holy ashes made from ingredients like tamarind bark, ghee, flowers) are distributed as prasad to devotees. Some rituals resemble those in Hindu temples, e.g. modes of offering.
