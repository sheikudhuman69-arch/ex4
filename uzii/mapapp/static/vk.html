Some of the resident and common species there include: Black-headed Ibis, Glossy Ibis, Night Heron, Indian Cormorant, Little Cormorant. In winter (October–April), many migratory species arrive, including Garganey, Northern Shoveler, Pintail, Rosy Pastor, etc. Around 15 species are known to breed in this habitat. Over 25 species roost here during the winter months. --- Conservation & Threats The area was proposed to be declared a Biodiversity Heritage Site (BHS) by the Tamil Nadu State Biodiversity Board to give it more protection. There was a planting of babul trees (Acacia) in 1996 by the Social Forestry division; many of these trees were cut down around 2007-08. Only about 20-30 % remained after some protection efforts.
